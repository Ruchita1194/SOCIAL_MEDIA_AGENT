# Video Script: overwatering myths (TikTok)

**Hook:** Your monstera is dying and you don't even know why.

**Body:**
- Most people overwater without realizing it.
- The soil never fully dries between waterings.
- Roots rot silently until the leaves turn yellow.

**CTA:** Follow for more plant-rescue tips.

**Best platform(s):** TikTok - fast cuts and trending audio fit the myth-busting hook well

**Voiceover script:**
If your monstera looks sad, it is probably not thirst, it is drowning. Most people water on a schedule instead of checking the soil, and that is exactly what kills these plants. Stick a finger two inches down. If it is still damp, walk away.

**Video-gen prompt (Runway/Pika/Kling/Sora style):**
Close-up macro shot of a wilting monstera leaf under soft window light, slow dolly-in toward the soil, moody plant-shop background, shallow depth of field, 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds
